## Example A — "AI, Plainly" (English AI-explainer shorts)
- **Adjectives:** editorial, understated, plain-spoken, anti-hype
- **Identity:** paper-grain texture + serif wordmark. NO glowing circuits / robot heads / neon-blue AI clichés.
- **Reference sites:** Practical Typography (type rules), The Inspiration Grid (kicker + serif headline cards), Minimal Gallery (type + whitespace title cards)
- **Rules:** big title and subtitle never say the same sentence; text always separated from background; white cards get a baked-in dark bottom band for subtitles

## Example B — Chinese tech-explainer channel
- **Adjectives:** clean, confident, title-as-subject
- **Identity:** black background + restrained single-accent glow/particles (e.g. rainbow-gradient dotted type). Cover style must match the video's own look; never crop the subject to fit — extend the background instead.
- **Reference sites:** Dark Mode Design (black + white headline + tiny glow accent), posts.design ("headline is the visual" title cards), before.click (title-first vertical cards)
- **Rules:** title is the subject of the cover; copy must match the video content, no clickbait ambiguity
